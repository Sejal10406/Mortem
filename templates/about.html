THE FORENSIC CHARTER

ABOUT MORTEM

Turning completely unnecessary predictions into an unnecessarily official, clinically grave experience.

"Scientific? No.
Dramatic? Absolutely."

Every pencil, mug, charging cord, and sock quietly endures human brutality, academic panic, Monday mornings, and washing machine wormholes. Most humans do not care enough to calculate their remaining days. MORTEM establishes official forensic justice for the inanimate.

THE 8-STEP MORTEM PIPELINE

01.

Subject Identification

Cross-references the item with baseline mechanical lifespans cataloged in data/objects.json.

02.

Condition Calibration

Translates tactile integrity (0% Destroyed to 100% Mint) into physical degradation coefficients.

03.

Usage Impact Assessment

Measures operating cycles from gentle preservation (1.25x) to extreme stress testing (0.45x).

04.

Owner Responsibility Scoring

Judges human custody, penalizing abandonment in drawers and hazardous desk-edge placement.

05.

Rule-Based Lifespan Engine

Executes multi-variable multiplication combined with micro-entropy jitter to determine remaining survival days.

06.

Risk Stratification

Assigns authoritative statuses: SAFE (>75%), AT RISK (50-75%), CRITICAL (20-50%), or TERMINAL (<20%).

07.

Cause of Death Synthesis

Interrogates historical case patterns from data/causes.json to pinpoint the tragic final failure mode.

08.

Official Dossier & Death Certificate

Renders a verifiable autopsy case file and a notarized, printable Death Certificate with unique federal identifier.

SCIENTIFIC DISCLAIMER

MORTEM is an intentional parody and engineering demonstration developed for humor and educational purposes. While the calculations utilize authentic multi-tiered mathematical multipliers and controlled stochastic variance, no real pencils were physically harmed during software verification.
